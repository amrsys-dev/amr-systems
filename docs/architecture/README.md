# AMR Systems — Architecture Foundation

## Purpose
AMR Systems is the public entry point for an ecosystem of independent digital products. The site presents the ecosystem, explains each product honestly, and provides clear navigation to available product destinations.

## Product boundaries
- AMR 3D Constructor — furniture configuration and 3D design.
- AMR Vision — furniture model analysis and decomposition.
- AMR Cutting — sheet-material and profile cutting optimization.
- AMR Service Hub — service discovery and request matching.
- AMR Materials — research and development of composite materials.

Each product is an independent module. The public site must not imply that unfinished integrations or capabilities are already operational.

## Public site structure
1. Intro — short, skippable brand introduction.
2. Hero — concise AMR Systems positioning and primary navigation.
3. Ecosystem — how independent modules fit together.
4. Products — dedicated product summaries and destinations.
5. AMR OS — explanation of the modular ecosystem architecture.
6. About — company and development principles.
7. Contact — verified contact channels only.

## Design system
- Graphite / near-black foundation.
- Silver and restrained copper/bronze accents.
- Electric cyan used sparingly for interactive highlights.
- Preserve the approved AMR monogram asset; never redraw or alter the mark.
- Responsive layouts with mobile-first navigation.
- Arabic locale must support right-to-left layout.

## Localization
Initial locales: Russian (ru), English (en), Uzbek (uz), Arabic (ar).
Arabic pages use RTL direction; other locales use LTR.

## Delivery principles
- Build a small, reliable public-site foundation before adding complex animations.
- Keep product content data-driven and independently editable.
- Avoid claims about features, integrations, pricing, or availability until verified.
- Keep the public website separate from the internal product applications.
